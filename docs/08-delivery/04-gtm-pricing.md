# Go-To-Market & Pricing

## 1. Wedge sequencing (mirrors roadmap phases)

Phase 1–2 beachheads = the wave-1 packs: retail counters (POS-led), FMCG distributors
(beat-app-led), pharma (compliance-led), discrete manufacturers (GST-complexity-led),
professional services (self-serve digital-native). Geography: 3 launch clusters (one metro,
one tier-2 trade hub, one tier-3 belt) to prove the vernacular/offline thesis before spend.

## 2. Channels (priority order)

1. **CA/accountant partner program** — free multi-client console, filing-work automation,
   referral revenue share, co-branded client migration service; CA becomes hero, not
   casualty (principle 11). Target: every CA manages 50–500 SMBs.
2. **Device/POS resellers & Tally partners** — margin-carrying reseller SKU with
   provisioning portal; the existing hardware channel already visits every shop.
3. **Self-serve freemium** — content-led (vernacular GST/business literacy on YouTube/WA),
   free tier virality via shared invoices/payment links ("Powered by" footer).
4. **Trade associations & brand mandates** — distribution pack via FMCG brand DMS mandates;
   pharma associations; industry bodies.
5. **Marketplace/ONDC ecosystem** partnerships (phase 3+).

## 3. Pricing architecture (positioning: "Tally-price for a BOS")

| Tier | Price (indicative, per company/month, annual billing) | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | ₹0 | 1 user, full compliance singles (invoice/e-invoice/e-way/GSTR projections), 1 GSTIN, community support — a complete, honest, compliant micro-business tool |
| **Starter** | ₹499 | 3 users, POS 1 terminal, WhatsApp basics, bank import, all wave-1 pack features of one pack |
| **Growth** | ₹1,499 | 10 users, multi-branch/GSTIN, HR+payroll to 25 employees, AI standard quota, portals, API |
| **Scale** | ₹3,999 | 25 users, manufacturing/quality, field apps fleet, advanced AI, priority support |
| **Enterprise/self-host support** | custom | Consolidation, dedicated infra/schema, SSO/SCIM, SLA, partner implementation |

Rules: **no per-invoice/per-document pricing ever** (anti-goal); user-tier overflows priced
gently; AI metered by fair-use quotas with top-ups; packs don't cost extra (verticals are
the product, not upsells) — depth tiers do.

## 4. Open-core line (license decision)

Core platform + all modules + compliance engines: **AGPL-3.0** public repo (trust +
contamination-safe vs proprietary forks); commercial license for OEM/embedding; cloud,
partner marketplace revenue share, enterprise features (consolidation, SSO/SCIM, dedicated
tenancy), and support SKUs are the business. Compliance never paywalled (R2, principles 1/9).
Trademark policy protects brand while permitting community distributions.

## 5. Migration offense (the Tally playbook)

"Switch Saturday" program: CA-assisted guided migration (masters, openings, 2 comparative
months parallel-run) with certification checklist; Tally-bridge keeps the CA's muscle memory
during transition (export back to Tally format anytime — the trust bridge that ironically
prevents churn); Busy/Marg/Vyapar/Zoho importers per wave. Success metric: median migration
< 1 working day with CA sign-off.

## 6. Brand & naming (parked from vision §6)

Criteria locked (12-language pronounceability, trade-warm, .in/.com). Candidate space to
test with target users: Vyapar-adjacent Hindi roots (danger: genericide), Sanskrit-neutral
(e.g. "Sutra", "Setu" family), coined-modern. Decision gate: before Phase-1 partner
onboarding (rebranding 50 tenants is cheap; 5,000 is not). "Epic BOS" remains internal
codename until then.
