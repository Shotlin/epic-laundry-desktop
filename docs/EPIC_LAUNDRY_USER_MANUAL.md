# Epic Laundry: the everyday user manual

This guide is for a person working at a laundry counter, care floor, delivery desk, or store office. Read **Start here** first. Then open the section for the job you need to do. Your store may show fewer pages because your account only shows work you are allowed to do.

## Start here: the four things to remember

1. **Dashboard tells you what needs attention today.** Click a number to open the matching work list.
2. **Overview explains how the business is doing.** Choose a date range to change its charts.
3. **An order is a journey.** It starts at booking, moves through care and delivery, and ends when it reaches the customer.
4. **Check before you save.** Browsing, searching, and changing a draft are safe. Buttons such as **Book order**, **Save**, **Complete**, **Refund**, **Import**, and **Close shift** record a business action.

The everyday path is:

**Dashboard → Order booking → Store orders → Production → Pickup & delivery → Print centre → Reports/Overview**

### Sign in and find your way

- Sign in with the account your store owner gave you. Never share the password.
- On a wide screen, use the left menu. On a phone, open the menu button first.
- Pages open as a work area with a **Back** button. Use it to return to the Dashboard and choose your next task.
- Use the top search for an order number, customer name, or phone number. A page's own search only looks inside that page.
- A purple or coloured item with **View details** opens another page. A plain number without that wording is information only.
- If you cannot see a page or an action, ask the store owner about your account permissions.

## 1. Dashboard: what should I do next?

**Why it exists:** The Dashboard is the morning notice board. It helps you choose the next job without opening every page.

1. Read the four cards: **Collection amount**, **Order requests**, **Pending orders**, and **Upcoming delivery**.
2. Click a card to see its related report or filtered order list. The filter name stays visible on the destination page.
3. Use **New order** to start booking. Use **Search customer** to find a saved customer. **Send WhatsApp** opens a review step; it does not send a message by itself.
4. Check **Order pipeline** for Booking, Delivery, and Delivered. Each number opens the matching orders.
5. Check **Needs attention** for unassigned pickup, due deliveries, unassigned delivery, express work, or requests.
6. Use **Recent orders** to reopen an order by its number.

**Counter sales** means orders booked at the counter. **Online orders (est.)** is an estimate from the online channel. Do not add these numbers together as if they are both cash received.

## 2. Overview: what is happening in the business?

**Why it exists:** The Overview turns recorded orders, payments, and customer visits into pictures you can understand quickly.

The six coloured cards show **Revenue**, **Orders**, **Collections**, **Average order**, **Customers**, and **New customers**. Below them, full size charts show each subject. A single selected day shows the day's value clearly. A wider range shows a trend. An empty panel means there are no matching records for that date range.

| Picture | What it means |
|---|---|
| Revenue performance | Value of orders booked in the chosen order period. It is not the same as money paid. |
| Order volume | Number of booked orders over time. |
| Collection trend | Money actually recorded as collected. |
| Average order value | Booked revenue divided by the number of orders in that period. |
| Customer base | Customers with repeat visits versus customers with one visit in the chosen customer period. |
| New customer acquisition | New customer profiles created over time. |
| Booked orders and value | Order count and booked value shown together. |
| Lifecycle mix | How many orders are in each order state. |
| Visits by customer | Which customers visited most often in the selected period. |
| Service demand | Which laundry services brought in order value. |

There are **four separate date choices**: Orders Review, Collection, Customer Frequency, and New Customer. Changing one changes its related pictures; the others keep their own dates. **Custom** lets you enter a start and end date. Use the refresh button if another worker has just recorded new activity.

## 3. Counter: take an order

### Order booking

**Why it exists:** This is the counter's order builder. It calculates the quote while you work.

1. Search for the customer's name or phone. If they are new, choose **Add Customer**, check the details, and save the customer first.
2. Choose the delivery option: pickup, home delivery, or express, as needed. Set the expected delivery date.
3. Use category and service chips to find garments. Use the garment search for a specific item.
4. Press **Add** or **+** to add a garment. Change the quantity with **+** and **−**. Check the **Selected** list on the right.
   Weighed laundry starts at **1 kg**. Type the measured weight in its quantity box, such as **0.1 kg** for 100 grams. At **₹50 per kg**, 0.1 kg costs **₹5**. The selected list shows the unit and calculation; pieces and kilograms have separate totals.
5. Review prices and any item changes. A catalogue item without a usable price should not be booked until the price is fixed.
6. Open **Charge**, **Discount**, and **GST** selectors if they apply. The quote updates before booking. GST is a rule choice; do not type an invented tax rate. A choice that conflicts with store tax settings may require correction before booking.
7. Choose **Pay Later**, **Cash**, or **Online**. For Online, follow the payment review shown by your store; preparing a link is not proof that the money arrived.
8. Add remarks or order photos if needed. Check the customer, due date, garments, price, discount, tax, and payment one last time.
9. Select **Book order** only when everything is correct. Wait for the success message and order number. Do not click it twice if the page is still saving.

**Clear draft** removes your unsaved choices. It does not cancel an order that was already booked.

### Store orders & customers

**Why it exists:** This is where you find and work on orders that already exist.

- Search by phone, order number, or customer. Use filters to narrow a long list. **Clear** removes the search/filter choice.
- **Order History** shows the order's journey. Opening it does not change the order.
- **Order Summary** shows the full order and money details.
- Open a customer profile to see their contact details, visits, balance, wallet/reward history, and packages where available.
- Actions that change an order state are separate from reading it. Check the selected count and confirmation before a bulk update.
- If a dashboard shortcut shows **No Data Found**, check the filter banner and widen or clear the filter.

### Print centre

**Why it exists:** It produces a readable invoice/receipt and labels for garments or bags.

1. Find the saved order.
2. Choose the document or tag type and review the preview.
3. Check the customer, order number, items, quantity, and printer format.
4. Print or save a copy. A reprint does not create a second order.

## 4. Production: care for the garments

**Operations centre** is a map of the work: order pipeline, production, tracking, quality, delivery, routes, and print.

| Page | When to use it | Simple rule |
|---|---|---|
| Production queue | See work waiting for washing, cleaning, ironing, or finishing. | Start only work assigned to you; check the garment and order before moving it on. |
| Garment tracking | Scan or search a garment or bag identity. | Follow the item history before changing its physical location or tag. |
| Quality claims | Record a stain, damage concern, rewash, or customer claim. | Add evidence and choose the decision that actually happened. |
| Correction documents | Print the customer-facing note created by a finished quality decision. | It is a copy of a recorded decision, not an editor for the claim. |
| Returns | Record a customer request about a returned order. | A return request is **not** a refund. Record money returned through the payment/refund flow separately. |

If an item is missing, damaged, or counted incorrectly, stop and open the order and garment history. Do not move the whole order forward just to clear a queue.

## 5. Pickup & delivery: move the order safely

**Pickup & delivery** shows unassigned work, captain assignments, and order handoffs. **Route runs** groups several pickup or delivery stops into one trip.

1. Open the correct stage: pickup or delivery.
2. Check the address, due time, and order before assigning a captain.
3. In Route runs, choose eligible stops and build a route. Review the captain and order list before starting.
4. The captain starts the route and records each stop as complete. A skipped stop needs a reason so the team can follow up.
5. Use **Captain settlements** when money collected by a captain needs to be handed over and checked.

An assignment says who should do the work. It does not prove that the customer received the order. Check the recorded handoff state.

## 6. Finance & compliance: know where the money went

**Why it exists:** Orders say what customers bought. Finance says what was charged, paid, spent, owed, and checked.

| Page | Why you use it | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Finance & compliance | See revenue, cash, receivables, expenses, and any reconciliation warning together. | Choose a date range, read the number names, then open the source order, expense, or report before acting. |
| Store expense | Record money the shop spent, such as supplies or rent. | Search first; enter name, date, paid amount, receiver/invoice if known, then review and save. |
| Cash closing | Compare cash expected by the app with cash counted in the drawer. | Open a shift with a starting float; at close, count the physical cash and explain a difference. |
| Captain settlements | Check cash a captain brings back. | Match the captain's amount to their recorded work before confirming. |
| Statutory controls | Review tax and compliance records available to your role. | Ask the store's finance lead before changing a tax or statutory setting. |
| Finance setup | Set the store's accounting rules and planning values. | Owners should check every setting before saving because it affects later reports. |

**Three money words:** **Booked revenue** is the value of orders. **Collected** is money recorded as received. **Outstanding** is what customers still owe. They can be different numbers on the same day.

### Reports

Open **Reports**, choose a report, choose dates, then press **Apply Filter** where shown. Search narrows rows. PDF/print and spreadsheet actions use the current report context; inspect the date and filters before exporting.

| Report | Use it to answer |
|---|---|
| Invoice | What did we invoice, and what is paid? |
| Collection | What money came in, when, and by which method? |
| Order | How many orders and garments did we process? |
| Consolidated Invoices | What do several invoices show together? |
| Customer | What did each customer order or spend? |
| Customer Package | How are package purchases and uses moving? |
| Customer List | Which customers are on record? |
| Growth | Is business growing over the chosen period? |
| Discount | Which discounts were applied? |
| Expense | What did the store spend? |
| Balance | What remains to be paid? |
| Pickup Overview | What pickup work was recorded? |
| Rider Delivery | What did captains deliver? |
| Rider Collection | What money did captains collect? |
| Warehouse User Work | What work was assigned/completed by a warehouse user? This report may require activation. |

## 7. Customer programs: keep customers coming back

**Care packages** let a customer buy a defined set of laundry benefits. Search for the customer, choose an available package, review what is included and the price, then save the sale. When the customer returns, check remaining allowance before applying it to an order. Do not promise a benefit beyond the package balance or expiry shown by the app.

Customer wallet and rewards information appears on the customer work card when configured. A visible wallet balance is not a cash payment until the order's payment step records its use.

## 8. Business controls: set up the store

Most workers use these pages rarely. Owners and managers use them to define how the everyday screens behave.

| Area | What it controls |
|---|---|
| Store settings | Business name, address, defaults, UPI/invoice QR, and store options. |
| Garments & prices | The items the shop accepts and their service prices. |
| Categories, services, service units | The words and groups used to find garments while booking. |
| Store charges and discounts | Reusable choices shown in the order builder. |
| Store users | Staff accounts, roles, and branch access. |
| Store packages | Packages the store can offer. |
| Order number series | The pattern used for new order numbers. |
| Import prices, catalogue, customers | Bring spreadsheet data into the store after preview and validation. |
| Online orders, marketplace catalogue, marketplace sync | Work arriving from a connected online channel. |
| Platform controls, orders, finance, audit trail | Wider platform administration and evidence. |
| People & payroll | Staff and pay related work for authorized managers. |

For imports, download the sample file, fill it exactly, select the file, review errors and the preview, then use the final save action. Fix failed rows before trying again. Never use the production customer sheet merely to test the importer.

For settings, opening a page or changing an unsaved field does not mean the business setting changed. Look for **Save** or **Update**, then confirm the result. Keep duplicate names and inconsistent category spelling out of the catalogue.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| A page will not open | Check the network or local app connection, then refresh. If the whole localhost page says connection refused, ask the person who starts the local server to restart it. |
| You are signed out | Sign in again. Unsaved forms may need to be entered again. |
| No Data Found | Read the active date, status, and search filters. Clear or widen them. |
| A button is disabled | Finish the required fields, select an eligible record, or ask the owner whether your role can use it. |
| A price, discount, GST, or total looks wrong | Stop before **Book order**. Check the garment price, quantity, rule choice, and store settings. |
| Payment link prepared but no paid status | Wait for actual payment confirmation. Do not mark the order paid from the link alone. |
| Printer output looks wrong | Check the preview and paper format; keep the original order number for a safe reprint. |
| Cash drawer does not match | Count again and add the reason required by the close screen. Do not change old orders to force a match. |

## Practice safely

The **Sample workspace** uses demonstration data. It is for learning; reset only when you are ready to lose that sample work. A live store account contains real customer and financial information. Use fake customers and orders only in the sample workspace.

This guide describes the current screens. A connected online channel, real payment provider, physical printer, signed desktop release, and a store's own tax policy need their own setup and acceptance checks before staff rely on them for live business.
