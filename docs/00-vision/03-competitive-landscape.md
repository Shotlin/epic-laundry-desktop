# Competitive Landscape

> Positioning rule: we never win by being 10% better at someone's core; we win by being the
> only one that is complete + compliant + consumable simultaneously (vision §3).

---

## 1. Battle map

| Competitor | Strength (respect it) | Structural weakness (attack it) |
|---|---|---|
| **Tally Prime** (~2M+ paid) | CA-ecosystem monopoly, accounting trust, speed, offline | File-era architecture; weak CRM/HR/POS/e-comm; single-machine mindset; cloud bolt-on; no AI; English/Hindi only |
| **Zoho (Books/One)** | Polish, breadth, price, India brand | Suite of separate apps ≠ one system (sync seams); SMB-manufacturing thin; closed ecosystem; no self-host; vernacular shallow |
| **Vyapar / Khatabook / myBillBook** | Mobile-first simplicity, huge micro reach | Ceiling: not real ERP (no double-entry depth, HR, mfg); businesses churn out as they grow — **our free tier catches their graduates** |
| **Busy / Marg** | Distribution/pharma vertical depth, dealer channel | Dated tech & UX; same file-era limits as Tally |
| **Odoo (in India)** | Breadth, UX, partner network | India statutory via patchy partner modules; Enterprise pricing in ₹ stings; implementation-partner dependency |
| **ERPNext/Frappe** | Platform, India compliance, open source, Frappe Cloud | UX fragmentation, DIY posture, weak SMB onboarding, services-led growth ceiling |
| **SAP B1 / MS Dynamics / NetSuite** | Mid-market credibility | 10–50x our TCO; implementation months; SMB-hostile |
| **Deskera / Refrens / Swipe / ClearBooks etc.** | Niche velocity | Point solutions; compliance or invoicing only |
| **ClearTax/Cleartax GST, IRIS (GSPs)** | Compliance rails expertise | Not systems of record — potential **partners** (GSP) more than competitors |

## 2. The four incumbent architectures (why the gap exists)

1. **File-era desktop** (Tally/Busy/Marg): unbeatable latency + trust, unfixable collaboration
   /cloud/AI. They retrofit; retrofits leak.
2. **App-suite** (Zoho): each app polished, the *joints* are the product weakness — masters,
   permissions, reporting fragmented.
3. **Open-broad** (Odoo/ERPNext): platform right, last-mile India + consumability wrong.
4. **Mobile-micro** (Vyapar et al.): consumability right, ceiling low.

Epic BOS is deliberately the missing fifth architecture: **one platform, one data core, owner-
grade UX, compliance in kernel, open-core trust.**

## 3. Moat construction (in order of durability)

1. **Compliance engine currency** — a statutory rules team + fast release lane (arch 03 §5);
   every rule change shipped in days compounds trust that point competitors can't match.
2. **CA partner network** — the accountant console makes CAs multipliers (each CA ≈ 50–500
   businesses).
3. **Data gravity + AI flywheel** — extraction/matching accuracy improves with tenant-approved
   corrections; switching cost grows with history depth (while export-freedom keeps trust).
4. **Industry pack library** — each vertical pack is months of domain encoding; the catalog
   compounds like an app store.
5. **Open-core community** — self-host users → contributors → hiring pipeline → distribution.

## 4. Anti-goals (fights we refuse)

- No horizontal global launch before India depth is undeniable (English-market ERP is an
  ocean of well-funded corpses).
- No services-company drift: implementation stays partner-led; product stays self-serve-first.
- No per-document pricing (taxing compliance = betraying positioning).
- No closed data: full export forever (trust is the brand).
