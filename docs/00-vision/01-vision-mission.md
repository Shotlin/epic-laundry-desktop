# Vision & Mission

---

## 1. Vision

**Every Indian business — from a kirana counter to a thousand-worker factory — runs its entire
operation on one intelligent, compliant, affordable operating system.**

Not "software they also use." The operating system: where money, stock, people, customers,
taxes, and decisions live.

## 2. Mission

Build the world's most complete business operating system, India-first: GST/UPI/WhatsApp/
vernacular-native, offline-tolerant, AI-assisted, industry-switchable — priced so a shop can
start free and a factory never outgrows it.

## 3. The one-paragraph strategy

63M+ MSMEs run on Tally + Excel + WhatsApp + paper. Global suites (SAP/Oracle/Dynamics) are
unaffordable; Indian SMB tools (Tally, Busy, Marg, Vyapar) are deep in accounting but shallow
everywhere else; global open ERPs (Odoo, ERPNext) are broad but India-thin or UX-heavy. The
gap is a product that is simultaneously **complete** (ERP+CRM+HR+POS+e-comm), **compliant**
(GST/TDS/payroll statutory always current, in core), and **consumable** (owner-grade phone-first
UX in 12 languages, 15-minute onboarding from a GSTIN). We build that gap-filler as an
open-core platform (docs-first, then execution per `08-delivery/00-roadmap.md`), win trust in
5 wedge industries, then expand pack by pack until "Epic chala lo" is the default answer to
"business kaise manage karun?"

## 5. What "everything included" means (scope creed)

The user's brief demands totality. We commit to totality **via the platform, not via one
release**: the kernel + module + pack architecture (blueprint §3) is the mechanism by which
"everything for every industry" stays buildable, maintainable, and honest. The roadmap
(08-delivery) sequences it; the module catalog (03-modules) enumerates it; nothing in the
vision requires architectural rework to add later.

## 6. Naming

Working name **Epic BOS** (Business Operating System). Naming criteria for the eventual brand:
pronounceable in 12 Indian languages, .in/.com available, no Tally/Zoho phonetic collision,
carries "complete/epic/backbone" connotation. Candidates parked in 08-delivery/04 (GTM).
